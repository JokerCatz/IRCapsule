IR Capsule Wiring

ESP32-C3 Super Mini, ULN2003APG, 8× IR333C-A and 1–4× VS1838B. Everything you need to solder once: transmit, receive and an optional expansion header.

1 Transmit: GPIO5 → ULN2003 → 4 LED strings

No VCC pin: that is correct. The ULN2003 is an array of Darlington transistors with no supply input; pin 8 is its ground and each load brings its own supply. Capacitors: C1 (100µF electrolytic, + lead to 5V, striped side to GND) is recommended: the LEDs pull about 240mA in 38kHz bursts and C1 keeps the USB rail steady so the C3 and Bluetooth are not disturbed. C2 is optional because the ULN2003 needs no decoupling of its own. Ceramic caps marked 104 are 0.1µF; 102 is only 1nF, so do not substitute it. A ceramic cap has no polarity: one lead to the supply (5V or 3V3), the other to GND.

Orientation: notch up. The left column runs pin 1 to 8 downward, the right column pin 9 to 16 upward, so pin 16 is top right.

Current: per string about 5V − 0.9V (ULN drop) − 2×1.4V (LEDs) ≈ 1.3V across 22Ω, roughly 60mA; all four strings together peak near 240mA. A 38kHz carrier runs at about one third duty, so the average is lower. Use a USB supply rated at least 1A.

2 Receive: VS1838B in parallel on GPIO4

Check VCC: power the VS1838B from 3V3, never 5V. At 5V its output swings to 5V, above the C3's 3.6V pin limit, and can damage the GPIO. Before connecting OUT, confirm 3.3V on the VCC pin with a multimeter.

GPIO2, GPIO8 and GPIO9 are strapping pins: do not attach external parts. GPIO20 and GPIO21 are the default UART pins, but this project's serial output goes over USB, so they are free.

5 Checks before power-on

  • Multimeter, USB unplugged: 5V to GND must not be a short. The capacitors make the reading start low and climb; it only has to settle above 0Ω.
  • 3V3 to 5V: not connected.
  • ULN2003 pin 8 connects to the Super Mini GND; pins 1, 2, 3, 4 connect to each other and to GPIO5; pins 5, 6, 7 connect to ground.
  • LED direction in every string: short lead (−) toward the ULN. A reversed LED will not light or be damaged, but the whole string stays dark.
  • Measure 3.3V on the VS1838B VCC pin before connecting OUT.
  • After soldering, check with a phone camera (many front cameras show infrared): each LED appears as a pale violet-white dot while transmitting, which confirms every string works.
Tip: use a DIP-16 socket for the ULN2003APG so the spare can be swapped in. Identify the LED leads before soldering: the long lead is the anode (+).
